60 Install

One binary. Apple silicon and Intel.

$ brew install jefflaplante/tap/cull

Or straight from the latest release:

$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/jefflaplante/cull/releases/latest/download/cull_macos_universal.tar.gz | tar -xz && sudo mv cull_macos_universal/cull /usr/local/bin/

125 Offload

Copy the card. Every file checked.

$ cull offload /Volumes/LEICA\ M ~/Pictures --name "Forest portraits" --location "Forest Park, Portland"
  • Plans first: the folder, where its date came from, every refusal before a byte is written.
  • Reads the card once, hashing as it goes; reads every copy back from the disk.
  • --backup fills a second drive from the same read. --dry-run prints the plan.
  • Then scans the new folder, free: previews, faces, junk frames, sets.

250 Judge

Price it, then judge it. The model assesses; Go decides.

$ cull judge --estimate "$SHOOT"
estimate: 17 frames × ~6k in / ~1k out tokens ≈ $0.37 at list price (claude-sonnet-5-5)
ranking ≈ 3 call(s), $0.09 at list price
$ cull judge --backend claude-code --write-xmp "$SHOOT"
  • Sharpness at the eyes on a native-resolution crop; exposure, composition, eyes and expression; up to eight content keywords.
  • Near-identical frames are ranked side by side; the best of each set stay keep.
  • --batch halves the API price. --max-cost caps it. --resume picks up after Ctrl-C.

500 Review

Look for yourself. Your labels win.

$ cull review "$SHOOT"

A contact sheet opens in your browser. Every key saves to cull-labels.jsonl and the frame’s sidecar. Sets are boxed; − and + keep the top few by rank.

1000 Tune

Check it against you. Free, no model calls.

$ cull calibrate "$SHOOT"
$ cull decide --review-below-sharpness 7 "$SHOOT"
  • calibrate reports false culls (you said keep, it culled), missed culls and the review rate, and sweeps the thresholds.
  • decide re-applies the policy to the stored assessments. The policy is saved in the report; a flag you type overrides only its own setting.
  • Nothing should auto-apply until it’s calibrated on your own frames.

  • Each sidecar carries your stars, a colour label, cull:keep, and the keywords: portrait, smile, woodpile… plus Fall session and Forest Park, Portland, nested in Capture One.
  • Sort before importing: a catalogue loses track of frames moved afterwards. cull restore undoes it.
  • cull offload --verify "$SHOOT" re-checks every copy, even after sorting: 17 verified, 0 missing or different.

4000 Capture One

After import, push changes in.

$ cull apply-c1 --probe "$SHOOT" | osascript -
$ cull apply-c1 --run --exposure "$SHOOT"
  • Colour tags, keywords and your stars on images already in the catalogue. A dry run by default: read the AppleScript first.
  • --exposure sets the EV you chose in review. Model suggestions apply only where Capture One still holds the default.
  • Your DNGs are never modified. Every move is recorded and undone by cull restore.
